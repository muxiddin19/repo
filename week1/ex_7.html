Korean Bibimbap Recipe

What You Need

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • Spinach, bean sprouts, carrots
  • 1 egg
  • Sesame oil
  • Gochujang (Korean red pepper paste)

Steps

  1. Cook the rice and let it cool slightly.
  2. Blanch and season the vegetables separately.
  3. Warning: use medium heat when frying the egg.
  4. Place rice in a bowl; arrange vegetables and egg on top.
  5. Add gochujang and sesame oil, then mix everything together.
