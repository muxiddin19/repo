The café analogy
Think of it like this: You order coffee at a café. The barista hands you a paper cup with your name on it — that cup is the Promise. You walk away and sit down (your code keeps running). A moment later, either your coffee is ready (Fulfilled ✅) — the barista calls your name and you get your coffee. OR they ran out of oat milk (Rejected ❌) — they apologize. The cup (Promise) can only change state ONCE.
Key facts about Promises
A Promise can only change state once: Pending → Fulfilled, or Pending → Rejected. It can never go backwards, and it can never change from Fulfilled to Rejected.
fetch(url) always returns a Promise that starts as Pending. When the server responds successfully, it becomes Fulfilled. If there's no internet at all, it becomes Rejected.
Important gotcha: A server replying with HTTP 404 or 500 does NOT reject the Promise — the Promise is fulfilled (it got a response), but res.ok is false. You must check this yourself.
